Build With AI: Basics · Devpost Learn

Say your list in the dark. Your room lights up.

Photograph your room and paste a list you must learn in order. Loci puts each item on a real object along one route of up to 12 stops, with a strange little scene for each. Then the lights go out. Say or type the list back: code, not an AI, checks every answer, and each right one turns its stop green.

Stop 1 · The walk

The real app, from the first stop to every light on.

The one-tap example: twelve cranial nerves in a student room. A short walk with the lights on, then lights out and the list typed back, one stop at a time.

The answer checker, on real answers shared/score.ts

  1. typed occulomotor → Oculomotor

    Right. One slip in a long word.

  2. typed trochlaer → Trochlear

    Right. Two letters swapped count as one.

  3. typed auditory → Vestibulocochlear

    Right. Your list accepts it after “ / ”.

  4. typed 1. Optic → Optic

    Right. Numbering is ignored.

  5. typed Vitamin D → Vitamin C

    Wrong. Short words must be exact.

  6. said truck lear → Trochlear

    Right. It sounds alike: both are trklr.

Each line is what the real checker answered, run on these inputs (isRight for typing, spokenMatch for speech).

Stop 2 · Lights out

Learn with the lights on. Recall in the dark.

Three real screens from the same palace. Code never shows you the answer while you recall; a miss stays dark until you retry it.

Stop 3 · Who decides

The AI proposes. Code decides.

Two AI calls suggest objects and scenes. Plain code picks the stops, draws the route and checks every answer, so the score means what it says.

Code

Checks every answer

Plain code, never an AI. It forgives slips in long words, keeps short words exact, and takes your own accepted answers after “ / ”.

  • Optic5 letters1 slip
  • Oculomotor10 letters2 slips
  • Vitamin C7 + 1 letters1, then exact

AI

Finds real objects

Your photo is shrunk to 1280 px on your device, then a vision model names objects and boxes each one.

Stop 6 · Questions

Questions a judge would ask.

Does an AI decide if I'm right?

No. AI finds the objects and writes the scenes. A small checker in shared/score.ts decides green or red. “Vitamin D” never counts for “Vitamin C”.

Where does my room photo go?

It is shrunk on your device, then sent once to Gemini (or DeepSeek if Gemini is busy) to find objects. The server does not store it. Scenes are written from object names only.

What is prepared in advance?

The one-tap example, 12 cranial nerves in an AI-generated student room, was built ahead of time by the same helpers, so it opens with no AI call. Your own photo goes through the real AI; building usually takes 10–30 seconds.

Does voice work everywhere?

In Chrome and Edge, through the browser's own speech recognition. Everywhere else you type, and typing always works.

What lists work?

3 to 12 items, in order, 4 words or fewer each. Add other accepted answers after “ / ”, like Sodium / Na.

Will I still remember it next week?

Loci doesn't claim that. It shows recall in one sitting: the first-try score, the learning time and the recall time. Walk your saved palace again before the exam.
